House inToul Kork

Type
Homes
Where
Toul Kork, Phnom Penh
Size
New house on two floors, about 240 m²
Scope
Full interior, built-in joinery, lighting, planting of the court
Status
Invented for this concept site

The brief

Three generations moving from a flat into a new concrete house on a narrow plot. They wanted it to stay cool without the air conditioning on all day, and the grandmother wanted to see the garden from her chair.

What we changed

  1. Moved the stair so the front door opens onto light, not a corridor.
  2. Cut a court into the middle of the plan. Every main room now has a window onto it.
  3. Took the roof past the living-room glass, so no direct sun reaches the sofa.
  4. Pale floors that carry daylight deep inside. The lamps stay off until dusk.
  5. A shoe bench and a tiled wash spot at the door, where the family actually comes in.

Studio team: Founder and lead designer, project architect, site coordinator. No client names, on this site or any other.
