Joinery inTonle Bassac

Type
Furniture
Where
Tonle Bassac, Phnom Penh
Size
Built-in pieces for a three-bedroom flat
Scope
Furniture made to order with a local workshop
Status
Invented for this concept site

The brief

A couple with a lot of books and very little storage. They wanted furniture made to measure by people who would come back to fix it, and nothing that would swell in the wet season.

What we changed

  1. A floor-to-ceiling library wall in plywood with a hardwood edge.
  2. A floating vanity in the bathroom, lifted off the floor so it stays dry.
  3. A narrow wall cabinet for towels, open at the front so air moves through.
  4. Made with a workshop on the edge of the city, from drawings in Khmer and English.

Studio team: Furniture and sourcing lead, founder and lead designer. No client names, on this site or any other.

Materials

  • Reclaimed hardwood
  • Rattan and woven palm
  • Kampot stone

See them all in the materials library, or come and touch the samples at the studio.
