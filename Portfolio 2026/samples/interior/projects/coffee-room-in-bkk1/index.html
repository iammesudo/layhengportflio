Coffee room inBKK1

Type
Hospitality · Shophouses
Where
Boeung Keng Kang 1, Phnom Penh
Size
Ground floor of a shophouse, about 90 m²
Scope
Layout, bar, built-in seating, shading, lighting
Status
Invented for this concept site

The brief

A small roaster wanted a room on a busy street where people stay for an hour, not ten minutes. The front faced the afternoon sun and the bar blocked the window.

What we changed

  1. Moved the bar to the back, so the front is all seats and shade.
  2. A deep awning and a timber screen to keep the afternoon sun off the glass.
  3. One long bench along the side wall, built in, with storage under the seat.
  4. Woven pendants hung low over the tables, so the room feels smaller and calmer at night.

Studio team: Founder and lead designer, project architect, furniture and sourcing lead. No client names, on this site or any other.

Materials
