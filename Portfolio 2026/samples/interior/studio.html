Studio

A small studioin Boeung Keng Kang

Five people, one long table, a wall of samples and a workshop we trust on the edge of the city.

Who we are

Chantrea trained abroad, worked in other people's studios, and came home to Phnom Penh to design for the way people here actually live: heat, rain, three generations under one roof, shoes at the door.

Kambor is the Khmer word for lime. We use it on walls that breathe, and we plan the rest of a house around the same idea: shade first, air moving through, and materials that look better after ten wet seasons than on the first day.

We are small on purpose. The person who visits your home on the first day is the person who draws it and the person who walks through it with you at handover. We take on a few projects at a time, from one room to a whole building, in Phnom Penh, Siem Reap and Kampot.

What we hold to

Plainpromises

Things a client can check us on.

  • We say no when the brief is not right for us.

    If we are not the right studio, we tell you at the first visit, and often who might be.

  • Every quote is itemised.

    Furniture and materials at cost plus a clear handling fee, shown on every line.

  • No commissions from suppliers.

    We choose a tile because it is right for the floor, not because someone pays us to.

  • Drawings any builder can price.

    Keep your own contractor if you like. We hand them a full set and visit site.

  • Khmer and English, on paper too.

    Every drawing, quote and care guide in the language you prefer.

  • Things you can repair.

    Lime, oiled wood, cement tiles, woven cane. When something wears, it can be fixed here.

How we work

Six stages,one team

The same people from the first visit to the handover. Stages, fees and FAQ

  1. 01First talk and site visit

    Notes from the visit and a fixed design fee in writing.

    Free, about an hour
  2. 02Concept

    Mood boards, a layout plan and a first idea of cost.

    Usually two to three weeks
  3. 03Design development

    Drawings, 3D views and a materials board.

    Usually three to six weeks
  4. 04Build drawings and quotes

    A full drawing set and itemised contractor quotes.

    One to three weeks
  5. 05Site and build

    Weekly site visits and photo updates.

    A few weeks for a room, a few months for a house
  6. 06Handover and styling

    A snag list, a final walk-through and a care guide.

    The last week
