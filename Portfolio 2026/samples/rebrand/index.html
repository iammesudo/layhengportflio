Chapter01

Burberry

Changes
Aug 2018 · Feb 2023
2018 design
Peter Saville, for Riccardo Tisci
2023 design
Under chief creative officer Daniel Lee
Sector
Fashion

What changed

Mark
2018: no symbol, the name alone, plus a new monogram.2→2023: the Equestrian Knight returns, a mark first developed in 1901 and carrying the Latin word "Prorsum", meaning "forwards".1
Type
Bold, tightly set sans-serif capitals.→Serif capitals with small, sharp serifs. Closer to the pre-2018 wordmark in spirit, but heavier and less widely spaced.
Colour
Black.→Black, plus a coloured version of the 2023 logo listed on Wikipedia.6
Tone
Quiet, pared back and modern.→Archive-led and British. Coverage at launch called it "archive-inspired" and a "return to British eccentricity".45
  1. 1Burberry plc, Burberry History (archived). Knight developed 1901, trademark 1909.
  2. 2GQ, Burberry Has a New Logo, 2 Aug 2018.
  3. 6Wikipedia, Burberry, Logos.

Why

In March 2018 Burberry named Riccardo Tisci its chief creative officer. A few months later he presented a new logo and monogram designed by the English graphic designer Peter Saville.32 It replaced a logo that had been in use since 1999.6 The sources I could check do not record the company's own reason for the change.

In September 2022 Daniel Lee, previously creative director of Bottega Veneta, was named as Tisci's replacement.7 In February 2023 a new logo and branding brought the Equestrian Knight back.4 Speaking about his debut collection, Lee said: "It was exciting to try to find the narrative of Burberry. And I wanted to go back to the idea of functionality, men and women on the go, and clothes that will last, that are not too precious."8

  1. 3Vogue, Riccardo Tisci Rebrands Burberry With a New Peter Saville Logo, 2018.
  2. 7Vogue, Riccardo Tisci Is Out at Burberry, and Daniel Lee Has Been Hired to Replace Him, 28 Sep 2022.
  3. 8FashionNetwork, Burberry: Daniel Lee debuts by bringing back Britishness, 2023.

What happened next

The launch campaign featured British musicians and models including Shygirl, Liberty Ross and Skepta.9 The knight logo is still the one in use, listed on Wikipedia as "since 2023", and Lee remains chief creative officer.6

  1. 9Dazed, Shygirl, John Glacier and a swan front Daniel Lee's first Burberry campaign, 6 Feb 2023.

My take

The 2018 wordmark was easy to read and held up well at small sizes, but with the name covered it could have belonged to several fashion houses. The knight gives Burberry something nobody else owns, with more than a century of use behind it. I think the return is the stronger long-term asset, as long as there is a simplified version of the knight for very small sizes, where its fine detail will fill in.

Opinion · Loem Layheng

Chapter02

Jaguar

Change
19 Nov 2024
Agency
Not named in the sources I could check
Tagline
"Copy Nothing"
Sector
Cars

What changed

Jaguar described the change as a new typeface, a new colour palette and a new device mark, in a direction it called "exuberant modernism".12

Mark
The leaping cat, long known on Jaguar cars as the "Leaper" mascot.3→No cat in the new wordmark. A separate new device mark sits alongside it.1
Type
Wide, evenly spaced capitals: JAGUAR, set under the leaping cat.→A thin, geometric wordmark that mixes capital and lowercase shapes: J, a, G, U, a, r.
Colour
The earlier palette.→A new palette. The wordmark file itself is a dark grey. The Type 00 concept car that followed was shown in "Miami Pink" and "London Blue".7
Tone
Classic British luxury.→Art-world and fashion-led. The concept car was revealed at Miami Art Week, not at a motor show.7
  1. 1BBC, Jaguar unveils new logo and branding ahead of electric-only future, 20 Nov 2024.
  2. 2Dezeen, Jaguar unveils "unique and fearless" rebrand for "complete reset", 21 Nov 2024.
  3. 7Wikipedia, Jaguar Type 00.

Why

On 19 November 2024 Jaguar released an advert showing its new logo and branding, ahead of a relaunch in 2026 as an electric-only brand.1 A year earlier its owner JLR had set out plans to move Jaguar further upmarket after years of poor sales, as a lower-volume maker competing closer to Bentley and Porsche.3 Jaguar's own announcement was titled "Fearless. Exuberant. Compelling. This is Jaguar, reimagined".4

  1. 3Wikipedia, Jaguar Cars, citing Automotive News Europe.
  2. 4Jaguar Media Newsroom, press release, Nov 2024.

What happened next

The change was openly controversial. It drew criticism online, including from Elon Musk, Stephen Colbert and Nigel Farage, and critics said it pushed away Jaguar's traditional customers.56 Others called it a marketing success because it put Jaguar in front of a worldwide audience.8

On 4 December 2024 Jaguar revealed the Type 00 concept, the first car to carry the new logo and badge. It divided opinion in the same way.7 Jaguar stopped building combustion cars, with the last F-Pace made on 19 December 2025, and its sales fell to a small fraction of earlier levels in 2025. Wikipedia puts that drop down to the pause in new car production rather than to the rebrand itself.9 In May 2026 Jaguar said the production car previewed by the Type 00 would be called the Type 01.7

  1. 5Financial Times, Jaguar's rebrand has divided opinion. Is 'Project Roar' the road to success?, 14 Dec 2024.
  2. 6The Guardian, What Jaguar's radical rebrand reveals about the shift to electric cars, 4 Dec 2024.
  3. 8Forbes, Jaguar Rebrand Is Absolute Genius – Here's Why, 23 Nov 2024.
  4. 9Wikipedia, Jaguar Cars, citing Autocar India and The Independent.

My take

I understand wanting a clean break when the product itself was being replaced, and the new wordmark is certainly distinctive. My concern is legibility: mixing upper and lower case shapes makes the reader work, and that is a real cost on a badge read from across a street. I would have carried one familiar cue, such as the leaping cat, across the change as a bridge for existing owners.

Opinion · Loem Layheng

Chapter03

Mastercard

Changes
Jul 2016 · Jan 2019
Design
Mastercard and Pentagram, as credited on the Commons file page6
Circles since
1969
Sector
Payments

What changed

Mark
Interlaced circles with horizontal stripes where they meet.→Two flat circles that simply overlap. In 2019 the circles became the whole logo.1
Type
"MasterCard" in bold italic, with a drop shadow, set inside the circles.→"mastercard" in lowercase, set below the circles. The company also changed the written name from "MasterCard" to "mastercard".3
Colour
Red and yellow with shading.→Flat red and yellow, with a third colour, orange, where they overlap.
Tone
Embossed, 1990s card hardware.→Flat and quiet, sized for screens as much as for plastic.
  1. 1Wikipedia, Mastercard, Branding. Logo captions 1996 to 2016, 2016 to 2019, since 2019.
  2. 3Forbes, The Story Behind Mastercard's New Logo, 14 Jul 2016.

Why

The overlapping circles go back to 1969, when Interbank combined the two overlapping circles of the Western States Bankcard Association with the name "Master Charge". It became MasterCard in 1979, and card designs have kept the circles ever since.21

In July 2016 Mastercard introduced a new identity and corporate logo.3 In January 2019 it removed its name from the logo, leaving only the circles.45 Wikipedia does not record the company's own explanation. The trade coverage it cites framed the 2019 step as going "name-free", with "pitfalls and positives".4

  1. 2David L. Stearns, Electronic Value Exchange, Springer, 2011, p. 28.
  2. 4Advertising Age, "From "Priceless" to Wordless", 21 Jan 2019 (print, no link).
  3. 5Fortune, Mastercard Drops Its Name From Company Logo, 7 Jan 2019.

What happened next

The symbol-only logo is still in use. Wikipedia's infobox shows it as the logo since 2019, with the symbol itself dating from 2016.1

  1. 6Wikimedia Commons, File:Mastercard-logo.svg. Author field: Mastercard Inc. and Pentagram Design.

My take

This is the change I would point to as an example of doing less, well. Removing the stripes and the shadow made the circles work at the size of a phone wallet icon without losing anything people recognised. Dropping the name only works because the same two circles had been used for about fifty years; I would not advise a young brand to copy that step.

Opinion · Loem Layheng

Chapter04

Airbnb

Change
Jul 2014
Design
DesignStudio, as credited on the Commons file page4
Symbol
"Bélo"
Sector
Travel, marketplace

What changed

Mark
No symbol, the name alone.→A symbol, the "Bélo": one continuous line that can stand on its own, for example as an app icon.2
Type
A puffy, rounded script with a white outline.→A plain, rounded lowercase sans-serif.
Colour
Sky blue with a light-to-dark blend.→One flat coral pink.
Tone
Friendly, early web.→Calmer and more grown-up, closer to a global consumer brand.
  1. 2The Next Web, Airbnb updates design and introduces controversial new Bélo logo, 16 Jul 2014.

Why

In July 2014 Airbnb revealed a redesign of its website and mobile app and introduced a new logo at the same time.1 Airbnb's explanation is in the coverage Wikipedia cites (Entrepreneur, "Airbnb, Why the New Logo?"), which I could not open, so I have left it out rather than repeat it second-hand.3

  1. 1Wikipedia, Airbnb, History.
  2. 3Entrepreneur, Airbnb, Why the New Logo?, 17 Jul 2014.

What happened next

The launch coverage cited on Wikipedia described the new logo as "controversial".2 The Bélo is still Airbnb's logo, more than ten years on, and is the one shown in its Wikipedia infobox today.1

  1. 4Wikimedia Commons, File:Airbnb Logo Bélo.svg. Author field: DesignStudio.

My take

The Bélo swapped a friendly but generic script for a symbol that can sit alone on a small square, which suited a redesign of the app. It is simple enough to draw from memory in one stroke, and I count that as its main strength. The "controversial" label at launch shows the risk of an abstract symbol: until people learn what it stands for, they bring their own readings to it.

Opinion · Loem Layheng
